# Submitting your work

Submitting is the only save you have: when you submit, the lab stores your work, interprets the text and decides its state in a single step.

## Steps

1. Run your program and copy **all** the text it printed.
2. In *Submit work*, fill in the name, date and description, and paste the text.
3. If you want, ask to **preview**: your figures are drawn in the browser and nothing is stored. If your browser cannot draw in three dimensions, the preview is not available, but you can still submit.
4. Submit. The lab rebuilds your figures in the order they appear, starting at **0**.
5. Read the findings: each one tells you **which figure and which field** has the problem.

The text is stored **exactly as you pasted it**: it is not corrected or reordered. If something is wrong, it is wrong in your program.

## The two kinds of finding

| Finding | Meaning | Does it block the submission? |
|---|---|---|
| **Validation error** | The text could not be interpreted at that figure and field | Yes: the work stays in **Draft** |
| **Warning** | The value your program declared does not match the one derived from the dimensions | No: the work moves to **Pending** with the warning visible |

A warning shows **both values**, declared and derived. That formula mistake is what the lab exists to show.

You can fix and resubmit as many times as you need. Your submissions are in [My work](consultar-mis-trabajos.md).
