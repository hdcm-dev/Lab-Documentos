# Signing in to the lab

Enter your email and password in the sign-in form and submit it. Once in, the lab takes you to the panel for your role: students see their own work; the administrator sees the whole class.

## Before you start

- Your account must be **enabled**. A newly registered account stays **pending** until the administrator enables it.
- If you were given a **temporary** password, the lab will ask you to change it before letting you do anything else. See [Changing your password](cambiar-la-contrasena.md).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A newly registered account can't sign in | Wait for the administrator to enable it |
| You forgot your password | Tell the administrator: they reset it and hand you a temporary one in person |
